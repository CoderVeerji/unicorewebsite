Applications

Where to use it

01 / Long-haul road freight

Multi-axle haulage trucks

Maintains full oil pressure during long highway runs under intense summer heat. Keeps pistons and ring grooves free of heavy carbon deposits between scheduled oil drains.

02 / Agricultural operations

Farm tractors in field work

Protects cylinder walls when pulling heavy rotavators and disc harrows through dusty soil. Prevents internal engine wear during seasonal peak harvesting when tractors run all day without shutoff.

03 / Commercial city transport

Buses and delivery trucks

Guards engine bearings through heavy stop-and-go city traffic and extended idling at loading bays. Prevents sludge build-up in crankcases during humid monsoon stop-start driving.

Engine protection

What it does

01

Maintains strong oil film under high load

Stops metal parts from rubbing together when the truck is carrying heavy payloads up steep road climbs.

02

Keeps pistons and rings clean

Prevents soot and black sludge from settling inside the oil passages and sticking piston rings in the cylinders.

03

Protects bearings from acid and rust

Neutralises harmful combustion acids produced during diesel fuel burning so internal crankshaft bearings do not pit.

04

Resists oil thickening and breakdown

Does not evaporate or turn thick and tar-like during high summer temperatures on long commercial highway journeys.
