Lubricants built for machinery running in hard Indian conditions.

Unicore Lubricants manufactures dependable oils and specialty greases built directly for Indian road corridors, farm fields, and factory floors. Indian machinery operates under unforgiving conditions: intense summer ambient heat, pervasive road dust, continuous heavy loads, and long working cycles without sudden rest. Our products are made to shield rotating gears, protect sliding metal contacts, and hold steady fluid film so motors and hydraulic systems run reliably.

We make our products for regional stockists, counter owners, mechanic workshops, commercial transport fleets, and industrial plant maintenance buyers. Instead of complex claims, we focus on steady batch quality, durable containers, clear markings, and dependable dispatches so our stockists always receive containers ready for trade.

PROCESS

How we make it

Every product batch moves through three straightforward stages from base fluids to finished sealed packs.

[Photograph: Inside blending area with temperature-controlled mixing kettles on light ground]
01

Controlled blending

Base fluids and performance agents are combined in measured batches with continuous mechanical mixing and uniform heating to ensure even fluid body across every drum.

[Photograph: Physical batch sample verification showing clarity and flow check]
02

Batch checking

Samples are drawn from each finished blend to confirm fluid weight, visual clarity, and steady flow before releasing the batch to the container fill station.

[Photograph: Container filling line, foil seal application, and cap fastening]
03

Tight packing & sealing

Finished fluids and specialty greases are filled into clean retail bottles, trade buckets, and heavy barrels with tamper-evident seals to protect stock during road transit.
