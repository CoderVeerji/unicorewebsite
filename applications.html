APPLICATIONS

Dependable lubrication for machinery working across India.

From heavy line-haul transport traversing national corridors to tractors plowing dense rural soils and factories running round-the-clock assembly shifts, Indian operating cycles demand unyielding fluid integrity. Unicore oils and functional greases are engineered specifically to shield rotating gear assemblies, combustion chambers, and fluid circuits against extreme summer ambient heat, pervasive road dust, and uninterrupted heavy loads.

Commuter motorcycles and delivery fleets endure grueling stop-start urban cycles, dense traffic congestion, and prolonged idling in intense summer heat. Unicore two-wheeler engine oils and tire protectants safeguard high-revving engines, wet clutch systems, and tires against excessive wear, thermal degradation, and road debris.
