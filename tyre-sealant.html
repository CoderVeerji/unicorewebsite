Continuous puncture protection while the vehicle keeps moving.

Action Inside The Tyre

How it works

The mechanical sealing process begins the second a sharp object pierces the rolling tread.

01

Spreads across tread

As the wheel turns, centrifugal motion spreads the thick gel evenly over the entire inner face of the tyre crown.

02

Rushes into wound

When a nail punctures the rubber, the escaping compressed air immediately forces the gel directly into the puncture opening.

03

Packs tight plug

The solid bodies carried in the gel wedge tightly together inside the channel, forming a solid mechanical plug through the casing.

04

Holds full pressure

The plug seals flush with the tread face and holds working air pressure while the vehicle continues its trip without stopping.

Product Limitations

What it does not do

This product is built for real working conditions. We state its exact mechanical boundaries clearly so workshops and fleet managers know what to expect.

Limit 01

It cannot seal sidewall cuts

The gel stays on the rolling tread crown through centrifugal force. It cannot protect the vertical tyre sidewall from kerb cuts or knife slashes.

Limit 02

It does not replace worn rubber

The gel protects against punctures through sound tyre casing. It cannot restore cracked, bald, or aged rubber that has run past its legal life.

Limit 03

It cannot fix bent steel rims

Air escaping from damaged bead seating or a hammered rim flange cannot be plugged by tread sealant. That requires workshop rim repair.
